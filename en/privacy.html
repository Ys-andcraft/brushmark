Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ys andcraft ("we") handles information in the iPhone application BrushMark (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any information about its users, including personal information such as names, email addresses or location. No account is required to use the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the information required for its operation (the App's settings, and the times at which the App is used, in order to determine when to show reminders) solely on your device. This information is not transmitted to any external party. It is deleted when the App is removed from your device.

3. Notifications

The App displays notifications created on your device only if you have granted permission. You may change this permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

4. Network Communication and Third-Party Services

The App does not communicate with any external server, and it does not use advertising, analytics or any other third-party services. Web pages such as this Privacy Policy, when opened from the App's settings, are displayed in your device's browser.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as necessary. Any revision takes effect when it is posted on this page.

6. Contact
