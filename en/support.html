Support

Common questions about BrushMark. For anything else, please email us.

How do I change the timer?
Open Settings from the ⋯ at the top right. Under Timer, use − and + to choose 5 to 60 seconds per spot. To brush without the timer, turn off "Move on automatically".
I can't hear the sound / I don't want it
Turn Sound and Vibrate on or off under Signals in Settings. Please also check your device volume.
How do I change the brushing order?
Under Brushing order in Settings, choose Suggested or Set my own. You can redo your own order with "Set it again".
How do I stop in the middle?
Tap the × at the top left; it turns into Stop. Tap again to go back to the start screen.
Can I use it in Japanese?
Yes. Choose 日本語 under Language at the bottom of Settings.
Does it keep records?
No. When you're done, you're done.

About this app

BrushMark shows you where to brush and for how long, as a guide. It does not check how clean your teeth are or assess the health of your teeth or gums. If you have any concerns about your teeth or gums, please see a dentist.
