Support

Frequently asked questions about BrushMark. If your question is not answered here, please contact us at the address below.

How do I change the timer?
Open Settings from the "⋯" button at the top right. Under Timer, use "−" and "+" to set between 5 and 60 seconds per spot. To brush without the timer, turn off "Move on automatically".
How do I turn off sounds or vibration?
Under Signals in Settings, turn off Sound and/or Vibrate. If you cannot hear any sound, please also check your device's volume.
How do I change the brushing order?
Under Brushing order in Settings, select Suggested or Set my own. You can recreate your own order with "Set it again".
How do I stop partway through?
Tap the "×" at the top left; it changes to "Stop". Tap it again to return to the start screen.
How do I turn off reminders?
Turn off notifications for BrushMark under Notifications in your device's Settings app.
How do I switch the language to Japanese?
Select "日本語" under Language at the bottom of Settings.
Does the App keep a history?
The App has no feature for displaying a usage history.

About This App

BrushMark provides guidance on where and for how long to brush. It does not check how clean your teeth are, nor does it assess the condition of your teeth or gums. If you have any concerns about your teeth or gums, please consult a dentist.
