A toothbrushing app that remembers where you've brushed.

About

While we brush, our minds are usually somewhere else, and we end up wondering whether we already did that side. BrushMark remembers where you've brushed for you. It moves you on one spot at a time, at the pace you choose, so you finish on time without brushing too long. No records, no sign-up, no ads, and no data collected. In English and Japanese. Free for iPhone.
