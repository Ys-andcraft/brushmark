Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This page explains how BrushMark ("the app") handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect any information about you, including personal information. There is no account.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (color, timer, rounds, signals, brushing order and language) are saved only on your device so they are there next time. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app also deletes these settings.

Network, ads and third-party services

The app does not connect to the internet. It uses no ads, no analytics and no third-party services. Pages such as this one, when opened from Settings, are shown in your browser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
